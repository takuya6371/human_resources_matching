Photographs and identity

Japanese employers expect a formal photograph on your Japanese CV, they pay attention to the person behind it, and they trust a profile more when the name on it has been checked.

Passport photograph

30 × 40 mm Drag to move your face

Drag the photo, or use this slider. Japanese document photos want the whole head with a little space above it.

This goes straight onto your Japanese CV. What is expected there is narrow:

  • Head and shoulders, facing forward, taken in the last three months
  • Plain pale background, no hat, no sunglasses
  • A business suit is usual; a neutral expression is normal, a broad smile is not

A few casual photographs

Up to three. At work, at something you are good at, with people you like. A short caption does more than the photograph alone — say what is happening.
